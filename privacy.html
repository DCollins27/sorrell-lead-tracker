Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClosePilot collects and how it's used.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email, business name, and a securely hashed password.
  • Lead data: whatever you enter into your pipeline (names, companies, deal values, notes, etc.).
  • Billing information: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details — Stripe shares back only a customer/subscription ID and status.
  • AI assistant prompts: when you use the AI features, your prompt and relevant pipeline data are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response.

2. How we use it

To operate your account and the pipeline dashboard, process billing through Stripe, generate AI responses through Anthropic, and improve the Service.

3. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run ClosePilot: our database host, Stripe (billing), and Anthropic (AI features). We do not sell your data.

4. Data retention and export

Your leads remain in your account until you delete them or close your account. You can export all your lead data to JSON at any time from the dashboard.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text). Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. Access to your leads is scoped to your account only.

6. Your choices

You can update your account details, export your data, or cancel your subscription at any time from within the app. To fully delete your account and data, contact support.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
